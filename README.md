Ohm’s law :-
V = I * R 
Here ‘V’ stands for voltage , ‘I’ stands for current and ‘R’ stands for resistance.
 If ‘R’ is very small for the given ‘V’ and ‘I’ will be large.

Lab Platform Familiarization and Connections

A switch is a tool that is used in a circuit to complete or disconnect it. 
There are many types of switches like:
•	Push button 
•	Throw switch 
•	Slide switch (This has 3 terminals. It can be in two positions.) 
•	DIP switch 
VCC is for positive volts and GND is for zero volts.
